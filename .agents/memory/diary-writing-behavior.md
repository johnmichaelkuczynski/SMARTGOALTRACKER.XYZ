---
name: Diary writing and interaction rules
description: Product expectations for long-form diary writing, explicit interaction, draft safety and evidence-based diary analytics.
---

Enter must insert a paragraph break, never submit or clear writing. Interaction must be explicit. The writing area must be substantial, not a tiny composer under empty conversation space.

**Why:** The user reported that Enter made their entry disappear and prevented substantial multi-paragraph writing.

**How to apply:** Preserve whitespace and per-entry drafts across reloads, dates, same-day entry switches and mode changes. Only deliberate entry submission may move prose into the conversation; requests for interpretation must not become diary prose or clear a draft. Keep responses in the same unified diary box.

Interactive Diary is on by default. Its replies stay within one concise paragraph of at most 120 words. Conventional writing remains available. Diary Analytics is a distinct view for real writing statistics and evidence-dated interpretations of the authenticated user's saved entries, not fabricated mood scores.

**Why:** The user requested an interactive diary that can discuss the nature of their entries, along with a dedicated Diary Analytics section, while retaining the earlier short-response preference.

**How to apply:** Separate authored writing from assistant output and questions when computing writing statistics. Do not mistake one entry for a trend or claim comprehensive coverage when analyzing excerpts.
